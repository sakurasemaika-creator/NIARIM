More ways to discover
Beyond new arrivals and rankings, you can find works by keyword, tag, or author.
Search by keyword
Search for a word that interests you to find related works.
Browse by tag
Find works by tags for genres and topics you're interested in — follow what's trending or dig deeper into your favorite genre.
Find by author
Found a work you like? Check out the other works that author has posted. Find your favorite creators and follow their new work.
No login needed just to browse
Drawing and animating, plus browsing the Work Plaza, new arrivals, rankings, and an author's work list, are all available without logging in. Posting requires signing in with a Google account. You can post up to 1 work a day on the free plan, or up to 3 a day with Premium.

Bookmarks

More
Even more fun, even more convenient
Grow tags together, keep a video playing while you browse elsewhere, or breeze through works in your spare moments — extra touches that make the Work Plaza even more fun to use.
Tags anyone can help curate
Tags aren't just for the poster — anyone can add or remove them. Posters can lock important tags so others can't delete them. Tap a tag to narrow the list down to works that share it.
Follows and reposts
You can follow the creators you like. Switch to the "Following" tab to catch up on just their new work. You can share other people’s work with "Repost", and when someone you follow reposts a work it appears in your Following tab too, labelled "Reposted by …". Only you can view the lists of users you follow and works you have bookmarked. Follower identities and lists are hidden. Following and follower counts are displayed. Following, bookmarking, or reposting does not send the other user an individual notification about that action.
Floating preview & work details
Tapping a work card doesn't jump straight to another screen. A small preview window appears in the corner, letting the video keep playing while you browse elsewhere. Drag it around or resize it freely. Tap "Details" to see the full work-detail screen with post date, author, tags, and bookmark count.
Vertical mode
Filter the gallery by All, Portrait only, or Landscape only. Portrait works can switch to a full-screen, vertically scrolling viewer with one tap.
Post your own work.
Finished work can be posted to the Work Plaza. Let someone else see what you made. Someone might love it enough to bookmark it, and it might even land in the rankings. Free members can post up to 1 piece a day; Premium members up to 3. Once you've made something, it's time to share it with someone.
Logging in stays simple, too.
There's no separate NIARIM account to register for. Sign in with a Google account to post and bookmark works. You don't need to log in at all just to browse.
From people to work. From work to people.
See someone's work, and a new idea is born. Publish what you made, and someone else sees it in turn. That feeling of "I want to try making something too" carries into the next piece. Your work becomes someone's favorite. And whoever saw it goes on to make something new of their own.
